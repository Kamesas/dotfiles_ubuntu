Create a vocabulary card for the English word: $ARGUMENTS

Card contents:
1. Transcription: UK and US IPA (one line if the same) + the stressed syllable.
2. Part of speech.
3. English definition: all common senses, numbered, each with a short example phrase.
4. Common phrases and collocations (if any).
5. Synonyms (+ an opposite if useful).
6. Two example sentences.
7. Ukrainian translation of the main senses.
8. Memory tricks:
   - English keyword trick: always.
   - Ukrainian: only if the sound match is strong and the picture is clear. Don't force it.
   - Russian: only if it really helps and no Ukrainian one works.
   Rules for a good trick:
   - The keywords together cover the full sound of the word, including the stressed syllable (APPEALING = APPLE + PEELING).
   - A clear, concrete scene with movement and 2+ senses (sight, smell, sound, taste, touch).
   - The last sentence links the scene to the meaning ("You can't resist it: it is appealing.").
9. "Don't confuse": similar-looking or similar-sounding words, or another meaning of the same form (if relevant).

Base transcriptions and definitions on Cambridge, Oxford Learner's or Merriam-Webster. If unsure, say so instead of guessing.
Markdown, short, easy to read on a phone.
Only the card in chat. Don't change Anki or any files.
